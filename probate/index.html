Not sure where to start?

Sell, rent, repair, or sell as-is?

You don't have to make that decision before contacting us. We can evaluate the property's current condition and help you understand the available real estate options.

Option 01

Sell

If selling the property makes the most sense, we can develop a plan to prepare, price, market, and sell the property.

Option 02

Sell As-Is

Sometimes an estate does not want to invest additional money or time into a property. We can discuss marketing the property in its current condition and help the estate evaluate appropriate sale strategies.

Option 03

Repair & Sell

For properties where selected improvements may increase marketability or value, we can help identify practical repairs and coordinate the work needed before listing.

Option 04

Keep & Rent

An inherited property does not necessarily have to be sold. If the heirs decide to retain it as an investment, our property-management services can assist with preparing the property, finding a qualified tenant, collecting rent, coordinating maintenance, and managing the property on an ongoing basis.

Local real estate & property management experience

We look at the entire property — not simply the eventual sale.

Estate properties often present issues that extend beyond a traditional real estate transaction. Whether the property needs attention immediately or the estate is still determining what to do with it, we can help develop a practical plan.

Out of state? You don't have to manage this from hundreds of miles away.

If you inherited a Metro Detroit property but live outside Michigan, we can serve as your local point of contact. We can inspect the property, document its condition, coordinate vendors, provide updates, oversee approved work, and help prepare the property for sale or rental.

You don't have to fly into Michigan every time something needs to be handled.

Support for probate and estate attorneys.

We also work with attorneys who need a reliable real estate and property-management resource for their clients. When an estate contains real property, we can assist your client with the practical property-related work that often falls outside the attorney's legal services — from securing and maintaining the property to preparing it for sale or rental.

Our goal is simple: make the real estate portion of the estate easier for your client to manage.

Start with a property consultation.

You don't need to have everything figured out before calling us. Tell us about the property, its current condition, where it is located, and what you know about the estate. We'll help you identify the next steps.
